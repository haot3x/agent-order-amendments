# ERC-8505 work records for amended orders

Author: Ted (@haot3x)

[ERC-8505](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8505-evaluated-work-records/30447) posts one
feedback entry per escrow-settled task, with a record a reader can rebuild from the
escrow's logs. At the pinned revision a task is consistent only with exactly one
`funded` step and a terminal step that settles the funded amount. An ERC-8448 order
funded at 100 and amended to 180 pays 180, so it gets no entry even though it was
accepted and paid in full.

This experiment tests an optional `supplemented` step that closes that gap, as
[proposed](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8505-evaluated-work-records/30447/9)
and refined by Zealynx and kurosato in the
[replies](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8505-evaluated-work-records/30447/11).
It is a proposal under discussion. ERC-8505 does not define the step, and nothing
here changes the ERC-8448 signed format, ABI or fixed vectors.

## The step

| Step | Meaning | Required | Optional |
|---|---|---|---|
| `supplemented` | both parties replaced the task's terms; any price increase is held with the rest | `task`, `amount` | `brief` |

Rules added to the pinned reader:

- Every `supplemented` step comes after the task's `funded` step and before any
  `delivered` step. ERC-8448 does not apply an amendment once work is submitted.
- The terminal step settles the funded amount plus every supplement.
- A supplement above zero is a transfer of that amount from the funded step's
  client to the vault in the same transaction, backed by its own `Transfer` log.
  A supplement of zero (terms changed at an unchanged price) moves no money and
  needs no log.
- The brief in force at delivery is the last one logged by a `funded` or
  `supplemented` step before delivery. A verdict is read against that brief.

Every other rule (outcomes, worker, challenges, logs after the terminal
transaction, malformed logs) is the pinned reader's own code, called unchanged.

## Source events

`OrderAmendmentApplied` has `intentHash` as its first indexed parameter and
`orderId` is a `bytes32`, so a descriptor cannot declare the ERC-8448 events
directly. The test-only `WorkRecordOrderFixture` mirrors the lifecycle with the
task id first, `task = uint256(orderId)`, using the shapes ERC-8505 suggests for a
new source plus one new event:

```solidity
event TaskSupplemented(uint256 indexed task, uint256 amount, bytes32 brief);
```

It is emitted inside the isolated application call, next to
`OrderAmendmentApplied`, so a failed top-up rolls it back with the rest. The base
contract gains empty internal hooks for this; its ABI is unchanged. `brief` is the
order's terms hash. Activation is the provider's own call and is declared as the
worker's act. The fixture's buyer is its only evaluator and it has no reject path,
so these vectors cover `accepted` and `lapsed` after assignment, not `rejected`.

## Vectors

[`vectors/erc8505-supplemented.json`](vectors/erc8505-supplemented.json) holds the
descriptor, the source's logs, the receipts of every money transaction, the
expected reading of six tasks and five cases a reader must fail.

| Task | Steps | With the step | Pinned rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| `price-increase` | funded 100, assigned, supplemented 80, delivered, verdict, paid 180 | `accepted` | inconsistent: settles 180 of 100 funded |
| `same-price` | funded 100, assigned, supplemented 0, delivered, verdict, paid 100 | `accepted`, amended brief in force | `accepted`, but the only brief is the superseded one |
| `two-amendments` | funded 100, assigned, supplemented 80, supplemented 40, delivered, verdict, paid 220 | `accepted` | inconsistent: settles 220 of 100 funded |
| `failed-top-up` | funded 100, assigned, delivered, verdict, paid 100 | `accepted`, no supplement logged | `accepted` |
| `never-amended` | funded 100, assigned, delivered, verdict, paid 100 | `accepted`, identical steps | `accepted` |
| `amended-then-lapsed` | funded 100, assigned, supplemented 80, refunded 180 | `lapsed` after assignment | inconsistent: settles 180 of 100 funded |

Must-fail cases: a supplement whose `Transfer` is missing from its receipt, a
same-price amendment rewritten to claim a supplement, a dropped supplemented log,
a supplemented log moved behind the delivery, and a payment one unit short of the
sum. Each case is a patch over the vector's logs and receipts with the error a
reader reports.

## Reproduce

```sh
npm ci --ignore-scripts
npm run test:work-records
npm run vectors && git diff --exit-code -- vectors/
```

Expected: 16 tests pass and the regenerated vectors are byte-identical.

## Limits

- The vectors come from a local Anvil chain (chain id 31337) with a disposable
  token. Unlike the ERC-8505 reference vectors they cannot be re-read from a
  public network.
- The reader in [`src/work-records-supplemented.mjs`](src/work-records-supplemented.mjs)
  reads steps, outcomes and transfers. It does not build the record file or the
  `giveFeedback` calldata, because a record format that carries the new step is
  ERC-8505's to define.
- Same-project evidence: the source, the descriptor and the reader extension have
  one author. The pinned reader in [`vendor/erc8505`](vendor/erc8505/README.md) is
  unmodified and its SHA-256 is checked by the tests.
- The fixture is not an audited escrow. Use only disposable test tokens.
